04

Enterprise Networking & Wireless Infrastructure

From core switching and campus routing to high-density Wi-Fi 6/7 and SD-WAN, we design, deploy, and optimize vendor-neutral enterprise network architectures engineered for high availability, security, and long-term scale.

  • Structured LAN, WAN & SD-WAN architecture
  • High-density Enterprise Wi-Fi 6/7 rollout
  • Core switch, router & gateway configuration
  • Vendor-neutral network consulting & strategy
  • Site RF surveys, heat-mapping & capacity planning
  • Redundant multi-path failover & VLAN segmentation
05

Data Centre Build & Structured Cabling

Turnkey data centre and server room engineering from the slab up — raised flooring, rack containment, certified copper/fiber structured cabling, and uninterrupted UPS power backup systems.

  • Data centre site planning, raised flooring & containment
  • Server rack, enclosure & airflow thermal planning
  • Certified Cat6, Cat6A & single/multi-mode fiber cabling
  • Online UPS sizing, battery banks & generator synchronization
  • Precision cooling, PDU integration & fire suppression
  • TIA/ISO standards compliance & as-built documentation
06

Cybersecurity, Network Defense & VAPT

A multi-layered defense architecture combining next-generation firewalls, zero-trust network access, continuous threat monitoring, and hands-on vulnerability assessment & penetration testing (VAPT) through to remediation.

  • Next-Generation Firewall (NGFW) design, deployment & hardening
  • Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing (VAPT)
  • Zero-Trust Network Access (ZTNA) & micro-segmentation
  • Network Access Control (NAC) & device trust enforcement
  • Compliance gap analysis (ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR)
  • Intrusion detection/prevention (IDS/IPS) & SIEM integration
  • Security policy governance & firewall rule audits
  • Ongoing threat monitoring & periodic health reviews
07

Smart Surveillance (CCTV) & Workplace AV

End-to-end electronic security and boardroom collaboration systems — IP surveillance networks, NVR/DVR storage, access control, and intuitive audio-visual conference room integrations.

  • High-definition IP & AI-powered CCTV camera deployment
  • Centralized NVR/DVR video storage, retention & remote viewing
  • Biometric & RFID access control integration
  • Boardroom, auditorium & hybrid conference room AV design
  • Wireless presentation systems, video walls & digital signage
  • Acoustic tuning, PA systems & automated AV control
08

Managed IT, AMC & Facility Operations

Comprehensive on-site IT staffing, 24/7 proactive infrastructure monitoring, and SLA-backed Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) to ensure zero downtime and peak hardware health.

  • 24/7 remote infrastructure & server health monitoring
  • SLA-backed Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)
  • On-site IT facility staffing & desk-side engineers
  • Hardware preventive maintenance visits & spare parts logistics
  • IT asset lifecycle management & software license tracking
  • Helpdesk coordination, disaster recovery & patch management
09

Unified Network & Data Center Monitoring (DCIM)

Single-pane-of-glass observability unifying multi-vendor network fabrics, SD-WAN edge nodes, and Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM) — delivering real-time SNMP/NetFlow traffic telemetry, rack thermal sensor monitoring, smart PDU/UPS health, and automated threshold incident workflows.

  • Real-time SNMP, NetFlow, sFlow & IPFIX network traffic telemetry
  • Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM) & thermal/environmental sensors
  • Automated network topology discovery, dependency mapping & device health
  • Smart PDU, online UPS battery banks & PUE energy efficiency tracking
  • Proactive threshold alerting, automated triage & instant incident routing
  • IT asset tracking, rack space utilization, 3D digital twins & SLA compliance reports
